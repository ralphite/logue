What goes, what arrives

Gone

  • Record, Keep and Ask as three separate verbs
  • The second recorder — there were two, behaving differently
  • The ask box that opened under the buttons
  • Four lists in one panel: transcripts, conversation, kept from this page, queued
  • The hidden state that remembered which box you were talking to

In their place

  • One box: type or talk, quote a passage, choose where it goes
  • One recorder: ✕ discard · ✓ insert · ↑ insert and send
  • One list, newest first, every entry the same shape
  • Skills under every entry, not only under dictation
  • Queued and failed recordings as entries, where they happened

One behaviour changes and it is worth saying plainly: today ⌘⇧K opens the panel listening, and ↵ sends what you said straight into the conversation. After this, ↵ only puts the words in the box — sending is ⌘↵ or the button. That is the price of being able to say a second sentence.

Three things only you can decide

1 · What does sending actually do?

Everything else follows from this one.

A · recommended

Sending keeps the note. Answering is the Ask skill underneath it, when you want one.

Your own words point here: “every submit has an ask under it” — so the ask comes after. One verb, and every entry has the same shape.

B

Sending asks the agent. What you say is a question by default.

Then keeping a thought without being answered needs its own button — and the three verbs come back.

2 · Does “into a Document” survive?

Deciding where the words land, before you say them.

Keep it — as a chip in the box

One chip on the composer's own row: Into · Panel notes. Not a section, not a step.

Not taste — data: of 33 things dictated into this workspace, 33 were never used again. Choosing the destination first is the one time it earned its place.

Drop it

Everything lands as a Source; you file it later in the app.

Fewer controls, and one fewer decision before talking.

3 · Does “save this page” keep a button?

The one act that needs no words.

A bookmark on the row

A small bookmark next to the mic — one press, page kept, entry in the list.

Visible, and it costs one 28px control on a row that has room.

Send an empty box

Nothing typed, nothing quoted, press send → the page is kept.

One control fewer, but it is a rule nobody can see. Not recommended.

Say A / keep / bookmark and it gets built in the six steps the proposal lists, each one its own commit and its own check. Nothing here is code yet.
