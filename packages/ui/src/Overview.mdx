# Logue, drawn at every level

Every product surface, every state it can be in — reviewable here without a
Host, a microphone, or an extension. Read the sidebar top to bottom; it is
ordered the way the work divides, smallest piece to whole journey.

| Level | What it holds | What to check there |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Foundation** | The tokens: color, density, elevation | Nothing else defines a color or a size |
| **Component** | One part, alone — a button, the recording player, an answer with citations | Every state of the part, including the ones with nothing in them |
| **Feature** | Parts assembled into one capability — Dictation, the bars on a page, the app's dialogs | The states that used to need a real failure to see: a refused model, a blocked microphone, silence |
| **Page** | The whole surface — the **real** `App` at a viewport, the **real** `Panel` at 360px | Layout truths panes cannot show: what competes, what collides, where the eye lands |
| **Journey** | One thing start to finish, in numbered frames | Whether the hand travels, whether what was true a frame ago is still on screen |

Two rules keep this honest:

1. **Pages mount the real thing.** The app pages are `<App />` told where it is
   by the address; the panel pages are `<Panel />` inside a story-sized fake
   `chrome`. Lookalikes drift the day after they are written.
2. **Only the wire is faked.** Fixtures answer `fetch` and
   `chrome.runtime.sendMessage`; the real request code, parsing and error
   handling run. A story that mis-reads the Host's shape breaks, which is the
   point of looking at it.
